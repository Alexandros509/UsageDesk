SQLite 3.50.4

The Windows bundle uses the unmodified SQLite library distributed with the
official CPython runtime. SQLite's deliverable library is dedicated to the
public domain by its authors.

Official copyright statement: https://sqlite.org/copyright.html
Official source: https://sqlite.org/src/

The source's traditional dedication reads:

The author disclaims copyright to this source code. In place of a legal notice,
here is a blessing:

    May you do good and not evil.
    May you find forgiveness for yourself and forgive others.
    May you share freely, never taking more than you give.
